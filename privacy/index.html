Privacy policy

Clink was built to need as little of your information as possible: no account, no ads, no tracking, no servers of our own.

Last updated 18 November 2026. Clink is made by Geyser Intelligent Information Systems LLC (“we”), 30 N Gould St, Sheridan, Wyoming 82801, USA.

What stays on your phone

  • Receipt photos and the text read from them. Clink reads receipts on your phone, using text recognition built into Google Play Services. The photo and its text are not sent to us or to Google, and Clink doesn’t keep the photo after reading it.
  • The bill you split: items, prices, who had what, tips.
  • Names you type for people at the table, and the friends list you choose to keep. These are saved only on your phone, so you can pick them next time. Delete a friend by pressing and holding their name; uninstalling Clink deletes everything.
  • Your language, if you pick English or Spanish in the app.

We never receive any of this. We have no servers that could.

What you choose to send

When you tap Send or Share, Clink opens your phone’s share menu with a short text message (for example, “your share is $38.06”). You choose the app and the people it goes to, such as WhatsApp or Messages. That app’s own privacy policy then applies to the message.

What Google’s text recognition sends to Google

Clink uses Google’s ML Kit, part of Google Play Services, to find the receipt in the photo and read its text. ML Kit processes the photo on your phone and doesn’t send the photo or its text to Google. It does send Google technical information about how it runs, for diagnostics and usage statistics, such as:

  • device information (manufacturer, model, Android version),
  • the app’s name and version,
  • identifiers for the device or the app’s installation,
  • performance figures (how long reading took), settings such as image size, and error codes.

Google says this data is encrypted in transit and not shared with third parties. See Google’s ML Kit data disclosure and privacy policy.

Location

Clink doesn’t use your location. To show the right currency, tips and tax, it reads the country code of your mobile network or SIM card (for example “US”), on your phone only.

Children

Clink isn’t aimed at children under 13, and collects no personal information from anyone.
